**1. Main Course: Vegetarian Moussaka**  

**Ingredients:**  
- 2 large eggplants, sliced into 1/4-inch rounds  
- 1/4 cup olive oil  
- Salt, to taste  
- 1 can (15 oz) chickpeas, drained and rinsed  
- 1 large onion, finely chopped  
- 2 cloves garlic, minced  
- 1 can (14 oz) crushed tomatoes  
- 2 tbsp tomato paste  
- 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon  
- 1/2 tsp ground allspice  
- 1/2 cup vegetable broth or red wine  
- Salt and pepper, to taste  
- 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese or nutritional yeast for a vegan option  
- 2 cups béchamel sauce (store-bought or homemade)  

**Preparation Instructions:**  
1. Preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C).  
2. Brush the eggplant slices with olive oil, sprinkle with salt, and place them on a baking sheet. Bake for 20 minutes until tender, turning once halfway through.  
3. In a large skillet, heat a bit of olive oil over medium heat. Add the onion and garlic, sauté until translucent.  
4. Add the chickpeas to the skillet, lightly mash some with a fork for texture.  
5. Stir in the crushed tomatoes, tomato paste, vegetable broth or red wine, cinnamon, allspice, salt, and pepper. Let simmer for about 15-20 minutes until thickened.  
6. In a baking dish, layer half of the eggplant slices, top with the chickpea mixture, then the remaining eggplant slices. Pour the béchamel sauce over the top and sprinkle with Parmesan cheese or nutritional yeast.  
7. Bake in the preheated oven for 25-30 minutes until the top is golden brown and bubbly.  
8. Allow the moussaka to cool for 10 minutes before serving.  

**2. Side Dish: Greek Salad**  

**Ingredients:**  
- 2 cups cherry tomatoes, halved  
- 1 cucumber, diced  
- 1 green bell pepper, sliced  
- 1 red onion, thinly sliced  
- 1/2 cup Kalamata olives  
- 1/2 cup feta cheese, crumbled or a dairy-free cheese for a vegan option  
- 1/4 cup olive oil  
- 1 tbsp red wine vinegar  
- 1 tsp dried oregano  
- Salt and pepper, to taste  

**Preparation Instructions:**  
1. In a large bowl, combine cherry tomatoes, cucumber, green bell pepper, red onion, and olives.  
2. In a small bowl, whisk together olive oil, red wine vinegar, oregano, salt, and pepper.  
3. Drizzle the dressing over the salad and toss gently.  
4. Top with crumbled feta cheese or a dairy-free cheese before serving.  

**3. Dessert: Baklava**  

**Ingredients:**  
- 1 package (16 oz) phyllo dough  
- 1 cup unsalted butter, melted (use vegan butter for a vegan option)  
- 2 cups walnuts, finely chopped  
- 1 tsp ground cinnamon  
- 1 cup sugar  
- 1 cup water  
- 1/2 cup honey or agave syrup for a vegan option  
- 1 tsp vanilla extract  
- 1 tsp grated lemon zest  

**Preparation Instructions:**  
1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C).  
2. Butter a 9x13 inch baking dish. Place 2 sheets of phyllo dough in the dish, brush with melted butter. Repeat until you have 8 sheets layered.  
3. Mix walnuts and cinnamon, sprinkle a thin layer over the phyllo.  
4. Add 2 more sheets of phyllo, butter, and walnuts, repeating until all ingredients are used, ending with 8 layers of phyllo.  
5. Using a sharp knife, cut into diamond or square shapes all the way to the bottom of the dish.  
6. Bake for 50 minutes until golden and crisp.  
7. While baking, combine sugar, water, honey or agave syrup, vanilla extract, and lemon zest in a saucepan over medium heat. Bring to a boil, reduce heat, and simmer for 20 minutes.  
8. Remove baklava from oven and immediately spoon the syrup over it. Let cool and serve.  

**Serving Suggestions:**  
- Serve the vegetarian moussaka hot, with a side of Greek salad for a refreshing contrast.  
- Present the baklava on a decorative plate with a sprinkle of additional chopped walnuts or a dollop of whipped cream or coconut cream for an elegant touch.  
- Pair the meal with a nice Greek wine such as Assyrtiko or a non-alcoholic option like sparkling water with a slice of lemon.  

**Explanation of Replaced Ingredients:**  
- The ground lamb or beef in the moussaka was replaced with chickpeas to provide a hearty and protein-rich alternative while maintaining the dish's texture.  
- Vegetable broth or red wine is used instead of beef-based liquids to keep the dish vegetarian.  
- Parmesan cheese can be replaced with nutritional yeast for a vegan option, maintaining the cheesy flavor.  
- Feta cheese in the Greek salad can be substituted with a dairy-free cheese for a vegan option.  
- For the baklava, vegan butter and agave syrup are used as alternatives to make the dish suitable for vegans without losing its sweet and rich flavor.  

Enjoy your delicious and healthy vegetarian Greek dinner!
